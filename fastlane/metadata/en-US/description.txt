Fitness Habit Tracker is a streak tracker that finds your fitness streaks automatically from the activity already in Apple Health, so there is nothing to check off each day. Three streaks are free, with no account, and it works on iPhone and Apple Watch.

STREAKS FROM YOUR HEALTH DATA
• With your permission, it reads your Health history and finds the streaks you already have, instead of starting every habit at zero
• Streaks for steps, exercise minutes, stand hours, active energy, workouts, mindful minutes, sleep, distance and flights climbed
• Three more built from your data: early steps, intensity (active calories per minute) and cardio minutes
• Daily and weekly streaks, with this day or week counted as live until it ends
• It picks the highest goal you have kept up, such as a 7,500 step day or a weekly workout count

BUILD YOUR OWN
• Custom streaks with your own metric, goal and daily or weekly cadence
• Track one workout type, such as running or yoga, instead of all workouts
• Step streaks can count only the steps in one hour of the day you choose, such as 6 to 7 a.m.

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your streaks
• The Apple Watch app shows your streaks, synced from your iPhone
• Watch face complications, including the corner style

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads and no cross-app tracking
• Apple Health is read only, and the app never writes to it
• Streak calculations run on your device

FREE AND STREAKS+
Three tracked streaks, three custom streaks, widgets and the Apple Watch app are free and do not expire. Streaks+ is an optional upgrade, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions include a free trial for eligible new subscribers, and the app shows its length before you start. Streaks+ adds:
• Unlimited tracked and custom streaks
• Auto-save, which keeps a streak alive when you miss a day
• Revive a streak that broke yesterday
• Planned freeze days for vacation, travel or sick days, which neither break nor extend a streak
• At-risk alerts before a streak breaks
• A history heatmap for each streak

Prices appear in the app before you buy and vary by region. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Fitness Habit Tracker is a fitness awareness tool, not medical advice.

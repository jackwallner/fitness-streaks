No account is required. Use an iPhone with existing Apple Health activity data and grant the requested read permissions. The app reads Health data to discover and track streaks for supported activity metrics. It does not write Health records. Results depend on the data and permissions available; an empty state on a device without Health history is expected. Health calculations run on the device.

Free users can track 3 streaks and build 3 custom streaks. STREAKS+ unlocks unlimited tracking, unlimited custom streaks, auto-save and travel freezes. The app includes calendar views, widgets and an Apple Watch companion. Open a locked STREAKS+ action or Settings to view the dismissible paywall. Monthly/yearly subscriptions offer a 7-day trial when eligible; lifetime is a separate one-time purchase. The purchase point shows the localized price, duration, renewal terms, Restore Purchases and legal links. Purchases and restoration use Apple StoreKit with RevenueCat and require connectivity. Test with a sandbox Apple account.

New in 1.3.3: saves and revives count correctly, and a revive bought the next day restores the run. Reminders follow the streaks being tracked, and streak days adjust after a time-zone change.

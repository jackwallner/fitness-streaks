A habit tracker should not become another habit to maintain. Turn the activity already recorded in Apple Health into visible proof that you're showing up.

CONNECT HEALTH. SEE YOUR CONSISTENCY.
With your permission, the app reads your existing Health history and finds streaks across nine supported metrics, including steps, workouts, exercise, sleep and mindful minutes. No daily check-off or duplicate activity entry is needed. What appears depends on your available history and read permissions.

START WITH THREE
Track three streaks free. Choose the health habits that matter to you and see how consistent you've been, instead of starting every habit counter at zero.

MAKE ROOM FOR MORE
The paid upgrade unlocks unlimited tracking and more ways to follow your progress. Calendar views, widgets and the Apple Watch companion help keep your habits visible.

Your Health records are read-only and calculations run on your device. The app does not write activity into Apple Health or guarantee a streak length. It is a fitness awareness tool, not medical advice.

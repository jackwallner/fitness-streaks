Discover fitness streaks already in your Apple Health history. Track steps, workouts and sleep without logging each day by hand.

Keep up to three streaks free. Streaks Pro unlocks unlimited tracked streaks. See your progress in calendar heatmaps, widgets and Apple Watch complications.

Explore nine Apple Health metrics, including steps, exercise, stand hours, energy, workouts, mindfulness, sleep, distance and flights climbed. Streaks depend on the history and permissions you provide. Calculations run on your device with read-only Health access. This is a fitness habit tool, not medical advice.

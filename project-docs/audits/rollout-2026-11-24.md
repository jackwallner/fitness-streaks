## Rollout S detail

Triggers found: one, `requestReview()` in `DashboardView.schedulePassiveReviewPrompt`, fired from `.reviewPromptPositiveMoment`, which only `StreakStore.signalPositiveMomentsIfNeeded` posts. There is no launch, scene-active, appear, tab, open-count or timer trigger. `ReviewPromptTracker.recordAppLaunch` only counts launches for the gate.

Good moments (both need the hero streak to be a run of 2 or more days or weeks):
1. Today's (this week's) goal newly met for the hero streak. A typical user reaches it in their 2nd or 3rd session: the first load builds the hero from Health history (3 days minimum), and any later open after hitting the daily target, or while hitting it with the app open, completes the unit. A cold launch restores the last snapshot first, so a goal met since the last open counts. Likely most daily users hit this on most days they open the app.
2. The hero streak grew (day or week rolled over with the unit complete). Roughly once per day or week for an active user who opens the app.

Gates unchanged: no ask before the 2nd cold launch, 30 days between calls (stamped when the call is made), opening the write-review page ends the passive asks, feedback never does, never during setup. About 3.5 s after the moment the dashboard re-checks that it is still on screen and that no sheet, alert, tutorial, trial pitch, paywall, picker or presented controller is up; if one is, the moment stays pending for the next one. No question, no pre-prompt, no button. Settings keeps its Rate row on `?action=write-review`. No analytics capture call exists in the app, so `rating_asked` is not logged.

Not measured: how many users reach each moment in session 2 or 3. The statement above is from reading the code, not from data.

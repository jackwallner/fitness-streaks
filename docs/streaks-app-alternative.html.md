## What is each habit tracker best at?

Streaks is best for to-do style habits, HabitKit for a free visual grid, Habitify for mixing manual and automatic habits, Gentler Streak for workouts with rest built in, and Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto for zero logging.

### Streaks

The original, and an Apple Design Award winner per its listing. You track up to 24 tasks a day, and tasks linked to the Health app, such as steps, complete on their own. It has an Apple Watch app with complications, iPad and Mac support, iCloud sync, negative tasks for breaking bad habits and timed tasks. It has the most ratings in this group. The trade-offs: it is a paid download, there is no free tier to try, and most habits outside Health still need a tap each day.

### Habit Tracker - HabitKit

HabitKit shows each habit as a grid of tiles, one per day, which is the main reason people pick it. Its listing says it is free with up to four habits, including streaks, statistics, notes and reminders, with no account, no ads and offline use. Pro adds unlimited habits and home screen widgets, as a monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase. The trade-off: the listing names iPhone only, with no Apple Watch app, and every habit is logged by hand.

### Habitify: Habit Tracker

Habitify mixes manual habits with automatic logging from Apple Health, Fitbit, Strava and Screen Time, so a walk or a run can tick itself off. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, and adds mood, notes, a timer and challenges. The trade-off: per its listing, the Apple Health and app integrations are part of Habitify Pro, and its rating is the lowest of the five.

### Gentler Streak Workout Tracker

Gentler Streak is a fitness app first. Its streak follows how active you are and lets you mark illness, injury or vacation without guilt, and it adds readiness, sleep analysis, workout tracking on Apple Watch and suggested workouts. Its listing cites a 2024 Apple Design Award and 2022 Apple Watch App of the Year. The trade-off: it is built around activity and recovery, so habits such as reading or journaling are outside its scope.

### Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto (my app)

This app reads your Apple Health history (read-only, on your device) and finds the streaks you already have in steps, exercise minutes, stand hours, workouts, sleep, mindful minutes and more, daily or weekly. There is nothing to check off, and it starts from your past data instead of day one. Free: three tracked streaks, a 7pm nudge for your top streak, widgets and the Apple Watch app, with no account. Its limits are real: it can only track what Apple Health records, so manual habits such as reading are out; the calendar heatmap, unlimited streaks and Grace Days are in the paid Streaks+; there is no iPad app; and it has no ratings yet.

## Which should you pick?

Choose Streaks if you want no subscription and Health-linked tasks. Choose an automatic tracker if logging every day is what breaks your streak.

- You want the classic to-do habit list, Health-linked tasks and no subscription: Streaks.
- You want a free tracker with a visual grid and are happy logging by hand: HabitKit.
- You want manual and automatic habits in one list, across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Watch: Habitify.
- Your main habit is working out and you want rest days built in: Gentler Streak.
- You already wear an Apple Watch and want your activity turned into streaks with zero logging: [Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto](https://jackwallner.com/ios/fitness-streaks/).
- You want both: pairing a manual tracker for habits like reading with a Health-based one for activity works fine.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free alternative to the Streaks app?

Streaks is a paid download, so the free alternatives here are HabitKit, Habitify, Gentler Streak and Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto. Each is free to download with an optional upgrade. HabitKit is closest for manual habits, and Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto suits people who want streaks from Apple Health data without check-ins.

### Which habit trackers read Apple Health automatically?

Streaks completes tasks linked to the Health app, per its listing. Habitify logs from Apple Health in its Pro tier. Gentler Streak builds on your Health activity, and Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto builds every streak from Health data with no manual habits.

### Which habit tracker apps work on Apple Watch?

Streaks, Habitify, Gentler Streak and Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto all support Apple Watch. HabitKit's listing names iPhone only.

### Do I need an account for a habit tracker?

Not always. HabitKit and Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto need no account. The Streaks, Habitify and Gentler Streak listings do not say either way, and Streaks syncs through iCloud.

### Is the Streaks app worth it?

It is worth it if you want a polished to-do style habit list with Health-linked tasks and no subscription. It is a paid download with an Apple Watch app, iPad and Mac support, iCloud sync and the most ratings in this group (4.81 from 27,350). The catch is that there is no free tier to try first, and most habits outside Health still need a tap each day.

### What is the best free app for habit tracking?

For manual habits, HabitKit is the strongest free pick here: its listing says up to four habits are free, with streaks, statistics, notes and reminders, no account and no ads. If your habits are activity that Apple Health already records, Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto tracks three streaks free with no check-ins. Habitify and Gentler Streak are also free to download, with optional upgrades.

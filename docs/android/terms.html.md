## Streak Finder Android Terms of Use

## Using Streak Finder

Streak Finder helps you understand personal activity habits from records shared with Health Connect. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Results depend on source data, permissions and device support. They are not medical advice or a substitute for professional care.

## Free and paid access

Free access tracks up to three streaks and permits up to three custom goals. Streaks+ adds unlimited tracking, full history, saves, freezes and proactive alerts. Subscription and lifetime plans, availability, localized prices and any trial terms are shown before purchase. Features that depend on Health Connect require supported data and permission.

## Billing and cancellation

Google Play processes Android purchases. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Google Play before renewal. Any trial converts to the displayed recurring price unless cancelled before the trial ends. Manage or cancel in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase with no renewal. Restore purchases from Settings or the paywall. Refunds are subject to Google Play policies and applicable law.

## Data and availability

Activity remains local, and uninstalling deletes local app data. Health Connect records are unaffected. Availability, imported history and notification timing depend on Android and the data sources you use. Keep any records you need independently. Read the Android privacy policy for data handling.

## Streak Finder Android Terms

Streak Finder helps you track activity habits. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or cure a condition. Data availability depends on the apps and devices sharing with Health Connect. Goals and streaks may change as source records change. Background refreshes and notifications depend on Android scheduling and permissions and are not guaranteed at an exact time.

Free includes up to three tracked streaks and three custom streaks. Streaks+ adds unlimited streaks, automatic missed-day saves, planned freezes, history and optional at-risk alerts. Prices, billing periods and any eligible trial are shown from the store before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Manage subscriptions in Google Play, Payments & subscriptions. A lifetime purchase is a one-time unlock without renewal. Refunds follow Google Play policies. Restore uses the same Google account. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google stores. Contact jackwallner@gmail.com.

# Streak Finder Android Privacy Policy

Web page: https://jackwallner.github.io/fitness-streaks/android/privacy-policy.html

## Streak Finder Android Privacy Policy

## Your activity stays on your device

Streak Finder reads only the Health Connect types you allow: steps, active calories, distance, floors, exercise sessions, sleep, heart rate and, when available, mindfulness. Activity values and calculated streaks stay on this device. They are never sent to us, RevenueCat, analytics services or advertising networks. The app does not write or delete Health Connect records. Android backup is disabled.

## Permissions and deletion

Each health type requires your permission. Optional past-history access allows longer imports; otherwise up to 30 days is read. Optional background access supports paid background refresh. Changing permissions clears the cached activity before the next import. Disconnect and delete local health data in Settings revokes access and clears local history and saved-run metadata. Uninstalling removes local app data. Your records in Health Connect are preserved.

## Purchases and diagnostics

RevenueCat processes purchase records, anonymous app-user identifiers, app/device information and paywall interaction attributes to provide Streaks+ purchase, restore and entitlement services. Google Play processes payments. No activity values are included. This app has no ads, no account requirement and no separate analytics SDK. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

## Notifications and external links

Optional notifications remind Streaks+ subscribers of at-risk goals. Links to support, coaching and store pages open external services with their own privacy practices. Sending feedback opens your email app and shares only the message you choose to send.

## Purpose and contact

Streak Finder is a personal habit tracker, not medical advice. Health Connect data is used only to provide activity streak tracking, never for advertising, sale, credit decisions or eligibility decisions. For privacy questions or purchase and app-interaction data deletion requests, email jackwallner@gmail.com. Updated October 18, 2026.

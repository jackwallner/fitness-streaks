## Streak Finder Android Privacy Policy

Streak Finder reads the Health Connect activity types you allow: steps, active calories, distance, floors climbed, exercise sessions, sleep, heart rate and supported mindfulness sessions. Activity values, goals, streak history and saves stay on your device. We do not send health data to a server. Optional past-data access supports longer histories. Optional background access supports automatic refreshes and saves. Health access is read-only and can be revoked per type in Health Connect. Changes to permissions clear local health history before importing allowed data again. Disconnect and delete local health data revokes access and clears local activity history and saved run data. Removing app storage also deletes local data. The app never deletes your Health Connect records.

RevenueCat and Google Play process purchase information, anonymous app identifiers, app version information and counts, dates and short labels about purchase screens. No health data or free text is included in purchase diagnostics. No ads or app account. Feedback opens your email app and includes only what you choose to send. Contact jackwallner@gmail.com.

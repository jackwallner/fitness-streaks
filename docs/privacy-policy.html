Streak Finder Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Streak Finder reads Apple Health activity only after you grant permission. It does not require an account and does not send HealthKit data to a server.

HealthKit

Read-only access may include step count, exercise minutes, stand hour, active energy, workouts, mindful minutes, sleep analysis, walking and running distance, flights climbed, and heart rate. The app uses this data on-device to compute and display activity streaks. It does not write to or modify HealthKit records, and does not use health data for advertising, marketing, data mining, or cross-app tracking.

Local storage and notifications

Daily activity summaries, widget and complication cache data, and preferences are stored locally. At-risk streak reminders are scheduled locally. No analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or third-party tracker is used.

Purchases

Streaks+ is sold through Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information, and limited technical context to verify subscriptions. It does not receive HealthKit data or your streak history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Retention, deletion, and permissions

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Revoke HealthKit permission in iOS Settings. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their policies.

Children and changes

Streak Finder is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.

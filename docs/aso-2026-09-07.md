# September 7, 2026 App Store positioning update

Target: Fitness Streaks 1.2.9 (149), all 50 App Store Connect text locales, release automatically after approval.

## Decision

Make automatic Apple Health streak discovery understandable through steps, workouts and daily habits. State the free three-streak limit, remove guaranteed streak-length claims and disclose current prices accurately.

## Evidence

ASC browser totals, June 9-September 6: 31 first-time downloads and 7,866 impressions. These are separate measures, not Apple's conversion-rate denominator.

July 13-26 had 5 downloads / 1,249 impressions; July 28-August 10 had 4 / 1,441. Hidden-streak messaging did not establish a measurable win. Astro streak finder #1 and fitness streak #19 support retaining the brand while explaining the use. The existing PPO treatment is inconclusive, not a winning creative.

The signed-in Apple Ads account has no campaign for this app. All 11 account campaigns were paused or on hold in the inspected view. No paid campaign or spend was started. Detailed ASC API reports are incomplete or privacy-suppressed and were not treated as zero-download evidence. Earlier om97/lm97 analyses were hypotheses, checked against current source data.

## Verification

Existing processed TestFlight build 149 is attached to the new review draft. The app test suite passed on a leased headless simulator; no production RevenueCat key was configured. No app source changes were needed for this positioning update. Strict upload validates exact character limits, all 50 locale sets, privacy and EULA links, then compares every uploaded field against ASC. Review notes describe current free, paid and hardware flows. Current subscription prices and eligible seven-day trials were verified; no pricing changes were made.

Approved screenshots are preserved. Text localization does not change the app's interface language or imply localized editorial content.

## Measurement

Keep this copy stable for at least 14 days after release/indexing unless Apple flags a factual issue. Compare 14- and 28-day impressions, first-time downloads, product-page conversion, trial starts per new download and paid conversion. Segment by storefront and source where volume permits; exclude simulator/test customers from monetization measurements. Record the actual release date before defining the post period. For Football, compare in-season weeks and account for kickoff rather than calling a preseason baseline a control.
